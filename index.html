Connect Google Classroom (one time)

To read your classes and homework, Shixy needs a free "Client ID" from Google. You only do this once. Follow the steps below, then paste the ID at the bottom.

Beginner's guide (about 10 minutes)

Google sometimes renames buttons, so the wording may be a little different on your screen.

  1. Open Google Cloud Console. Go to console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the same Google account you use for Classroom.
  2. Make a project. Click the project name at the top, press New Project, name it Shixy, press Create, then make sure it is selected at the top.
  3. Turn on the Classroom API. Open the menu, choose APIs & Services, then Library. Search for Google Classroom API, open it and press Enable.
  4. Set up the consent screen. In APIs & Services choose OAuth consent screen (or Google Auth Platform) and press Get started. Enter the app name Shixy and your email, choose External as the audience, and finish.
  5. Add yourself as a test user. On the same screen open Audience, find Test users, press Add users and type the Google email you use for Classroom. Save.
  6. Create the Client ID. Go to Credentials, press Create credentials, then OAuth client ID. Pick Web application as the type. Under Authorized JavaScript origins press Add URI and paste the address from the yellow box above. Press Create.
  7. Copy the Client ID. It is a long text ending in .apps.googleusercontent.com. You do not need the "client secret".
  8. Paste it below and press Save and connect. A Google window opens. If it says "Google hasn't verified this app", press Advanced, then Go to Shixy. This is your own app, so it is safe. Allow the read-only permissions.

Good to know

  • Shixy can only read your courses and homework. It cannot change anything in Classroom.
  • The address must match exactly. localhost and 127.0.0.1 count as different addresses.
  • School accounts: if Google blocks the sign-in, your school may have turned this off. Ask your teacher or IT admin, or try with a personal account.
  • Still saying "access blocked"? Check that your email is in the test users list (step 5).
